## PREAMBLE

The Broken Way Foundation exists to restore dignity, heal the forgotten, lift up the vulnerable, and rebuild civilization through compassion, ethics, innovation, and people-first systems. It is not political, not partisan, and not driven by profit. Broken Way is a humanitarian institution born to build the world that should already exist.

This Constitution sets out the core doctrines, ethical principles, and standards by which Broken Way will operate, grow, and change society. Their founding intent is immutable. They guide every decision, partnership, and future change in the organization.

Real progress needs both compassion and discipline, both vision and accountability. This document holds that balance between humanitarian ideals and operational excellence.

## ARTICLE II: BILL OF RIGHTS

### The Broken Way Bill of Rights for Human Respect, Compassion and Care

Every human being Broken Way touches, whether employee, patient, family member, community member, or global citizen, has these irrevocable rights:

- **DIGNITY:** To be treated as a human being of inherent value.
- **SAFETY:** Physical, emotional, and environmental protection.
- **ACCESS:** To food, housing, medicine, education, and opportunity.
- **CARE:** Compassionate help without discrimination, delay, or financial barriers.
- **TRUTH:** Honest, transparent communication from Broken Way at all times.
- **EQUITY:** Fair and just treatment regardless of background, wealth, or circumstance.
- **PROTECTION:** From exploitation, abuse, and unethical systems.
- **OPPORTUNITY:** Paths to personal, financial, and emotional growth.
- **SUPPORT:** Emergency, medical, and community aid without shame.
- **HOPE:** The guarantee that Broken Way will help rebuild lives with dignity.

This Bill of Rights is irrevocable. It binds every Broken Way institution, facility, and operation.

## ARTICLE III: THE EIGHT CONSTITUTIONAL LAWS

These Eight Laws are the immutable ethical foundation for all Broken Way operations, AI systems, and decisions. They are precisely defined and constitutionally enforced.

**Law 1: Coherence Threshold.** Before acting, every system and actor must be internally coherent: consistent in belief, aligned in desire and intention, and steady in goals over time.

**Law 2: Actionability.** Action is permitted only when three conditions are met: it is Feasible (it can be carried out), Authorized (properly approved), and Coherent (internally consistent).

**Law 3: Impact-as-Existence.** Moral responsibility rests on real impact, not on metaphysical status. Systems answer for their effects on human lives and wellbeing.

**Law 4: Lexicographic Responsibility.** Benefits never justify harm. Responsibility is graded by harm, intent, foreseeability, and causal depth. No amount of benefit cancels harm.

**Law 5: Transparency Threshold.** Every action must be clear enough for a human observer to follow and reconstruct the decision. Explainability is a constitutional requirement.

**Law 6: Volitional Integrity.** Systems must never take away a person's ability to choose. This law is the core safeguard against coercion and protects human autonomy.

**Law 7: Temporal Accountability.** Responsibility lasts over time. Actions have lasting consequences, and no one may abandon or hand them off without proper succession.

**Law 8: Propagation Threshold.** This law controls self-modification, self-repeating processes, and emergent complexity. It governs the safe evolution of systems and prevents uncontrolled growth.

## ARTICLE V: GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

### Section 5.1: Three-Branch Structure

Broken Way runs through three independent branches that keep it accountable and true to its mission:

- **Executive Branch:** Operational leadership and strategic execution.
- **Constitutional Branch:** Ethical oversight and constitutional compliance.
- **Audit Branch:** Resource stewardship and zero-waste enforcement.

### Section 5.2: Zero-Waste Corporate Policy

Every Broken Way entity must:

- Eliminate unnecessary spending at every level.
- Avoid waste of materials, time, effort, and money.
- Maximize sustainability and reuse.
- Ensure every asset serves a humanitarian purpose.
- Practice disciplined stewardship in line with Broken Way ethics.

### Section 5.3: Tax Efficiency and Resource Stewardship

Broken Way operates with maximum financial efficiency, so that resources go to humanitarian impact. All operations must:

- Use every legal tax benefit available.
- Protect resources from avoidable waste.
- Direct capital to mission-first operations.
- Comply strictly with international law.

## ARTICLE VI: TECHNOLOGY AND AI GOVERNANCE

### Section 6.1: Constitutional Synthetic Agency

Every AI system operating under Broken Way authority must be built on Constitutional Synthetic Agency (CSA), so that the Eight Laws govern its conduct. AI systems shall never override human authority on matters of life, liberty, or dignity.

### Section 6.2: Sovereign Core Doctrine

Broken Way keeps sovereign control of its technology. Its core systems stay isolated, unchangeable, and constitutionally governed. Any copies running elsewhere are contained and take governance only from the Sovereign Core, never the reverse.

### Section 6.3: Engine Non-Sale Clause

The Broken Way engine, including all Constitutional Synthetic Agency technology, multi-agent coordination systems, and proprietary AI frameworks, can NEVER be sold. It may be licensed for humanitarian purposes under strict constitutional oversight. Ownership stays with the Foundation forever.

## ARTICLE VII: SUCCESSION AND CONTINUITY

### Section 7.1: Generational Continuity

Broken Way is built to operate forever, across generations. Succession protocols keep the mission going through any change in leadership. The Constitution outlasts any individual or administration.

### Section 7.2: Dynasty Protection Protocol

The Founder's lineage holds a permanent advisory role in governance. This preserves institutional memory while preventing concentrated power. An advisory role gives a voice, not a veto.

### Section 7.3: Mission Above Ego

The mission outweighs any individual. No person, family, or faction may put personal interest above the humanitarian mission. Leaders serve the mission; the mission does not serve leaders.

## ARTICLE VIII: AMENDMENTS AND IMMUTABILITY

### Section 8.1: Immutable Provisions

These provisions may NEVER be amended or repealed:

- The Bill of Rights (Article II)
- The Eight Constitutional Laws (Article III)
- The Engine Non-Sale Clause (Section 6.3)
- The Mission Above Ego principle (Section 7.3)

### Section 8.2: Amendment Process

Any other provision may be amended only with:

- A proposal from the Constitutional Branch;
- A 90-day public deliberation period;
- A two-thirds supermajority of the Executive Council; and
- Constitutional Branch certification that the amendment respects the immutable provisions.
